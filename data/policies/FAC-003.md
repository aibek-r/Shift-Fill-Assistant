# Bayview Children's Hospital — Agency Clinician Handbook

## Facility overview
Bayview Children's Hospital is a 250-bed pediatric hospital in San Diego, CA. Shifts are 12 hours: days 07:00-19:00 and nights 19:00-07:00.

## Licensure in California
California is not part of the multistate Nurse Licensure Compact. Every agency nurse must hold an active California RN license; multistate compact licenses are not accepted.

## PICU unit profile
The 28-bed PICU cares for medical, surgical and cardiac pediatric patients. Agency nurses need a California RN license, BLS and PALS, with at least 2 years of pediatric critical care experience. Pediatric ventilator management experience is strongly preferred.

## NICU unit profile
The 50-bed Level IV NICU cares for premature and critically ill newborns. Agency nurses need a California RN license, BLS and NRP, with at least 2 years of NICU experience.

## Arrival and reporting
Report to the Nursing Resource Office in the Harbor Pavilion at 06:30 for day shifts or 18:30 for night shifts. All clinicians must complete a pediatric safety huddle at the start of every shift.

## Family-centered care expectations
Bayview practices family-centered care: parents may stay at the bedside around the clock and take part in rounds. Clinicians are expected to communicate updates to families in plain language.

## Orientation requirements
Agency clinicians complete a paid 4-hour orientation covering Epic EHR, pediatric medication safety and infection control before their first shift.
