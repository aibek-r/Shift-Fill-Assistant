# Staffing Operations Policies — All Facilities

## Credential compliance
A clinician may only be offered a shift when every credential required by the facility unit is active through the end of that shift. Credentials that expire within 30 days after the shift must be flagged so the clinician can upload a renewal.

## Outreach guidelines
Outreach messages are drafts for a coordinator to review; they are never sent automatically. Messages must state the facility, unit, shift dates and times with time zone, and a response deadline. Do not include pay rates in first-touch outreach unless a coordinator has approved them. Never mention other clinicians or their information.

## Candidate prioritization
When several clinicians are eligible, prefer candidates whose experience matches the unit's stated preferences, who have expressed a preference for the shift type (day or night), and whose credentials are not about to expire. Recommend more candidates than open positions only when coordinators asked for a shortlist.

## Privacy
Share only the minimum clinician information needed for the task. Contact details, license numbers and home addresses must not appear in recommendations or outreach drafts.
