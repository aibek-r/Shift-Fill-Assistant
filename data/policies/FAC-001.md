# St. Mary's Medical Center — Agency Clinician Handbook

## Facility overview
St. Mary's Medical Center is a 410-bed acute care hospital in Austin, TX and a Level II trauma center. Agency clinicians work alongside staff nurses on 12-hour shifts. Day shifts run 07:00-19:00 and night shifts run 19:00-07:00.

## ICU unit profile
The ICU is a 24-bed mixed medical-surgical unit with a 1:2 nurse-to-patient ratio. Agency ICU nurses must hold active RN licensure valid in Texas (a multistate compact license is accepted), plus BLS and ACLS, and need at least 2 years of recent ICU experience. CCRN certification is strongly preferred. Experience with CRRT and post-cardiac-surgery patients is highly valued because the unit receives cardiothoracic recoveries most nights.

## Emergency department profile
The ED sees roughly 180 visits per day, including pediatric patients. ED agency nurses need RN licensure valid in Texas, BLS, ACLS, PALS and TNCC, with at least 2 years of emergency experience. Triage experience is preferred.

## Night shift reporting
Night shift agency clinicians report to the House Supervisor office on the ground floor at 18:30 for badge activation on their first shift. The ICU night huddle starts at 18:45 at the 4 West nurse station. Bring a government-issued photo ID.

## Parking and arrival
Agency clinicians park in Garage C on 38th Street. Parking is free with an activated badge; on the first shift, take a temporary pass from the security desk at Entrance B.

## Orientation requirements
Every agency clinician completes a paid 4-hour computer-based orientation, including Epic EHR training, before their first shift. Clinicians with prior Epic experience can complete an abbreviated 2-hour module.

## Float policy
Agency ICU nurses may float only to the cardiac step-down unit, never to non-critical areas. ED nurses do not float.

## Cancellation policy
The facility may cancel an agency shift with at least 2 hours of notice before the shift start. Late cancellations are paid at 4 hours.
