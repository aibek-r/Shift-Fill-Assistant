# Lakeside Community Hospital — Agency Clinician Handbook

## Facility overview
Lakeside Community Hospital is a 180-bed community hospital in Denver, CO. Shifts are 12 hours: days 07:00-19:00 and nights 19:00-07:00.

## Medical-surgical unit profile
The 36-bed medical-surgical unit cares for orthopedic, general surgery and medical patients at a 1:5 nurse-to-patient ratio. Agency nurses need RN licensure valid in Colorado (multistate compact licenses are accepted) and BLS, with at least 1 year of acute care experience. Charge nurse experience is a plus because the unit often runs with one staff charge nurse.

## Telemetry unit profile
The 20-bed telemetry unit monitors cardiac patients. Agency nurses need RN licensure valid in Colorado, BLS and ACLS, with at least 1 year of telemetry or step-down experience. Strong rhythm interpretation skills are expected.

## Arrival and reporting
Report to the Staffing Office on Level 2 at 06:40 for day shifts or 18:40 for night shifts. Badges are issued by the Staffing Office.

## Parking
Use the visitor garage on Lakeside Avenue and validate your ticket at the Staffing Office.

## Orientation requirements
Agency clinicians complete a 3-hour orientation, including Meditech EHR training, during their first shift. The orientation is paid.

## Rest between shifts
Lakeside requires at least 8 hours of rest between any two shifts worked by the same clinician.
